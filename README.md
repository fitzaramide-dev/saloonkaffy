# Kaffy Saloon — Penthouse Hair Studio Website

A luxury, sleek, modern website for **Kaffy Saloon**, a boutique African hair braiding and weave studio located in a private penthouse in Santa Venera, Malta.

---

## 🌟 Features Included

1. **Editorial Luxury Design**:
   - Sleek Obsidian Black, Warm White, and Champagne Gold color palette.
   - Elegant typography pairing (*Cinzel*, *Playfair Display*, and *Plus Jakarta Sans*).
   - High-fashion visuals generated specifically for Kaffy Saloon.

2. **Hair Extensions "Bring or Buy" Policy**:
   - Dedicated banner explaining that clients can bring their own hair packs (BYOH) or buy from in-studio stock (X-Pression, extensions, bundles).
   - Direct button to ask Kaffy about hair prices and colors.

3. **Curated Services & Pricing Menu (From €30)**:
   - Knotless Braids (From €60)
   - Ghana Weaving & Feed-ins (From €40)
   - Classic & Boho Box Braids (From €50)
   - Weaves & Closures (From €50)
   - Simple Cornrows & Men's Braids (From €30)
   - Custom Hairstyles & Kids (From €30)
   - Every card has an instant WhatsApp booking link pre-filled with that specific style!

4. **Interactive Lookbook & Lightbox**:
   - Category filtering (All Styles, Knotless, Ghana Weaving, Box & Boho, Weaves).
   - Interactive modal lightbox: clicking any hairstyle lets clients zoom in and book that exact style.

5. **The Penthouse Experience Section**:
   - Highlights the 1-on-1 VIP comfort, quiet atmosphere, refreshments, entertainment screen, and sunny rooftop terrace.

6. **Location, Address & Hours**:
   - Exact address: `40, Anfield, Flat 2, Triq Brunzar, Santa Venera, Malta`.
   - "Copy Full Address" button with 1-click clipboard copy.
   - Direct Google Maps link.
   - Operating hours (Monday–Saturday 9:00 AM – 7:30 PM, Sundays by request).

7. **Client FAQ & Hair Prep Guidelines**:
   - Answers how to prep hair before coming, payment methods (Revolut, BOV Pay, Cash), and extension purchases.

8. **Floating WhatsApp Booking Button**:
   - Always visible on mobile and desktop for quick inquiries.
